How to play

  • 🃏 Tap two cards to flip them. If they match, they stay face up. If not, they flip back — remember where they were!
  • ⭐ Finish a level for 1 star. Beat the move target and the time target for 3 stars. Levels unlock one by one for each theme.
  • 👀 Early levels give you a Peek at the start. Higher levels add a timer target and a sneaky Shuffle that mixes the face-down cards after a few misses.
  • 👥 In 2 Players you take turns on one device. A match earns a pair and another go. Most pairs wins!
  • ⌨️ Keyboard: ←↑→↓ move, Enter/Space flip, P/Esc pause.
