How to play

  • Blocks fall from the top. Slide and spin them to fill whole rows.
  • A full row clears and scores. Clear 4 at once for a BLITZ!
  • Every 10 lines = next level = faster blocks. Chain clears for combos.
  • Game over when the stack reaches the top.
